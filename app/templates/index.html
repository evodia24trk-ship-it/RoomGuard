How it works

  1. Measure — place a smartphone in the room and record light (Lux) and sound (dB) with Phyphox, every 30 seconds.
  2. Predict — enter the two values on the Predict page (or send them to the /api/predict endpoint).
  3. Act — if the room is empty, switch off lights and AC to save energy; if occupied, leave them as needed.
