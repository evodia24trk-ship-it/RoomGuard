    Model & Dataset

    • Model: Decision Tree Classifier (scikit-learn), class_weight = balanced.
    • Features: Light_Lux, Sound_dB only — exactly the two ambience sensors in the assignment.
    • Target: Occupied (1) / Empty (0). Labelling follows the fixed standard in dataset/LABELING_STANDARD.md — headcount log, never the sensor value.
    • Public data: UCI Room Occupancy Estimation (864), accessed 23 Nov 2026, CC BY 4.0.
    • Private data: phyphox Light + Sound Meter, 30-second interval, manual labelling.
